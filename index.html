Research directions

A connected research program.

Across each field, the goal is the same: find compact, interpretable models and test them against meaningful physical or security benchmarks.

01 / LIGHT

AI for nanophotonics

How we work

From physical question
to defensible result.

The most useful result is one that survives a stronger baseline, a change in assumptions, and an independent check.

01

Model the system

Start with the physics and a clear computational task.

02

Generate evidence

Build simulation data and exact or classical references where possible.

03

Reduce complexity

Search for efficient representations and algorithms.

04

Audit the claim

Check accuracy, resources, robustness, and reproducibility.
