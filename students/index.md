## Areas for Student Research

Students may contribute to projects involving:

- Quantum computing and quantum algorithms
- Variational quantum simulation
- Quantum computational chemistry
- AI-assisted quantum circuit discovery
- Machine learning for nanophotonic inverse design
- Explainable AI for physical systems
- Scientific dataset analysis
- Quantum cybersecurity
- Quantum cyber deception and moving-target defense

## Skills You Can Develop

Depending on the project, students can gain experience with:

- Python programming
- Qiskit
- Machine learning
- Scientific computing
- Numerical simulation
- Data analysis and visualization
- Research reproducibility
- Scientific writing
- Conference presentations
- Preparation of journal manuscripts

## What I Look For

Prior expertise in every area is not required. More important are consistency, willingness to learn, careful documentation, and the ability to follow a technical problem through to a defensible result.

Students interested in joining the group should send a concise email describing their background, research interests, relevant coursework or programming experience, and the amount of time they can realistically commit.
