## Selected Publication

**M. Javani et al.**, “Reducing Computation Complexity in Optimization of Nanophotonic Structures Using Pruning,” *IEEE Photonics Technology Letters*, vol. 36, no. 4, 2024.

This work investigates neural-network pruning as a way to reduce the computational complexity of machine-learning models used in nanophotonic optimization.

## Current Research Manuscripts and Projects

- Reduced variational quantum representations for molecular systems
- Quantum simulation and reproducibility
- Intrinsic dimensionality of nanophotonic datasets
- Explainable machine learning for optical structures
- Physics-consistent learning for multilayer systems
- Quantum kernels for nanophotonic datasets
- Quantum cybersecurity and cyber deception
